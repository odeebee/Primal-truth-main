Working as a grounds keeper for the [REDACTED] museum woods means you must be fully prepared for any situation.
The basic equipment you must need are as follows:

    1. Steel cap toe boots 
    2. Cargo work trousers

    4. Radio

Equipment for more specialsed caretaking tasks will be provided for you in the tool shed in the woods.
Incorrect uniforms are a must and are for your physical saftey. The woods are a dangerous place.
